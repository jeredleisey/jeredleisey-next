The hard part of automation was never the tool. It was the map of the process: who does what, in which order, and what happens when a step fails. A bot that copies a broken process copies the break as well.

When the first agents arrived, the map still mattered. An agent can decide more on its own, but it needs to know where the edges of its job are. Write those edges down first.

Then test with the people who do the work today. They know the exceptions that no document names.
