title: Privacy
---

This page says what jeredleisey.com collects, why, and where it goes. It was last changed on 27 September 2026.

## When you read the site

The site counts page visits with Vercel Web Analytics. It uses no cookies and no personal identifier, and it shows me only totals. The site has no ads and no other trackers.

## When you sign in

You sign in with Google or GitHub. The site then stores:

- your name, your email address, and the address of your profile picture, as the provider gives them;
- the link to your Google or GitHub account, with the sign-in tokens that the provider issues;
- your sessions. A cookie keeps you signed in. With each session the site stores the IP address and the browser name, to protect your account.

## When you ask for access to a Project

The site stores the Project, the note you write, the dates, and whether I approved or declined the request. I get an email with your name, your email address, and your note.

## When you use the Jev prompt tester

For each Run, the site stores the model, the number of tokens, the cost, the time, and whether the Run worked. It does not store the text of your prompts or of the answers. To get an answer, your prompt goes to OpenRouter and to the model provider that OpenRouter uses, under their terms.

## Who handles the data

- **Vercel** hosts the site and counts the visits.
- **Neon** holds the database.
- **Resend** sends the email about Access Requests.
- **OpenRouter** sends your prompts to the model.
- **Google** and **GitHub** handle the sign-in.

I do not sell your data, and I do not share it with anyone else.

## Deleting your data

Send an email to [jered.leisey@gmail.com](mailto:jered.leisey@gmail.com) and ask me to delete your account. I then delete your account and everything the site stores about you.
